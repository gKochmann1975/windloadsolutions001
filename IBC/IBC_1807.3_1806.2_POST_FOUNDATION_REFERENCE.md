## §1807.3 Embedded Posts and Poles (scope)
Designs to resist axial + lateral loads using posts/poles as columns embedded in earth or in
concrete footings in earth shall be per §1807.3.1–1807.3.3 **or ASABE EP 486.3**. Typical
free‑standing **sign posts and fence posts use the NONCONSTRAINED case** (no rigid slab at grade).

---

## §1807.3.2.1 Nonconstrained  — Equation 18-1  ✅ VERIFIED
Use where lateral constraint is **not** provided at the ground surface (the usual sign/fence post).

```
d = 0.5 · A · [ 1 + ( 1 + (4.36 · h / A) )^0.5 ]

where   A = 2.34 · P / (S1 · b)
```

**Variables**
- **d** = required depth of embedment in earth, ft — **NOT to exceed 12 ft** for lateral‑pressure calc (beyond 12 ft → another method required)
- **P** = applied lateral force, lb (e.g., the wind force from the sign/wall calc)
- **h** = distance, ft, from ground surface to the point of application of P
- **b** = diameter of round post/footing, or diagonal dimension of square post/footing, ft
- **S1** = allowable lateral soil‑bearing pressure (psf) per §1806.2 **based on one‑third the embedment depth (d/3)**

> **Iterative:** S1 depends on depth (= lateral bearing value × d/3, capped), and d depends on S1 → solve iteratively.

---

## §1807.3.2.2 Constrained  — Equations 18-2 / 18-3  ⚠️ VERIFY FROM BOOK
Use where lateral constraint **is** provided at grade (rigid floor / slab‑on‑ground). Uses:
- **Mg** = moment in the post at grade, ft‑lb
- **S3** = allowable lateral soil‑bearing pressure per §1806.2 **based on full embedment depth (d)**

(The exact Eq 18‑2/18‑3 forms render as images on the code sites — confirm verbatim from the IBC
text. Not needed for typical free‑standing signs/fences, which are nonconstrained.)

## §1807.3.2.3 Vertical Load
Resistance to vertical loads → use the **vertical foundation pressure** from Table 1806.2.

---

## Table 1806.2 — Presumptive Load‑Bearing Values  ✅ VERIFIED (IBC 2021 / IL 2021; confirm 2024)
| Class of material | Vertical foundation pressure (psf) | **Lateral bearing (psf per ft of depth)** | Lateral sliding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Crystalline bedrock | 12,000 | 1,200 | 0.70 (coef. friction) |
| 2. Sedimentary / foliated rock | 4,000 | 400 | 0.35 |
| 3. Sandy gravel and/or gravel (GW, GP) | 3,000 | 200 | 0.35 |
| 4. Sand, silty sand, clayey sand, silty gravel, clayey gravel (SW, SP, SM, SC, GM, GC) | 2,000 | 150 | 0.25 |
| 5. Clay, sandy clay, silty clay, clayey silt, silt, sandy silt (CL, ML, MH, CH) | 1,500 | 100 | 130 psf cohesion |

**Notes / footnotes (confirm exact wording in book):**
- **Lateral bearing increases with depth:** the tabular lateral value may be increased by the
  tabular amount for each additional foot of depth, **up to a max of 15× the tabular value** (§1806.3.4).
- **Isolated poles** (flagpoles, signs) where a ½‑in. lateral motion at grade is acceptable and the
  structure is not adversely affected: lateral bearing values **may be increased** (§1806.3.4) — **verify
  the exact factor (commonly a 2× allowance) against the book.**
- Friction coefficient applies to dead load; cohesion applies to contact area per §1806.3.2.

## Build notes for the Signs/Fencing calculator
1. Feed **P** (wind force) and **h** from the sign/wall wind calc.
2. Inputs: post **b**, soil class (→ lateral bearing value), constraint condition (default = nonconstrained).
3. Solve Eq 18‑1 **iteratively** for **d** (S1 = lateral value × d/3, with depth increase + isolated‑pole
   factor as applicable; cap S1 per the 15× rule).
4. Outputs: required embedment **d** (flag if > 12 ft → needs alternate method), hole diameter, backfill
   note (§1807.3.3: concrete ≥2,000 psi in a hole ≥4 in. larger, or compacted sand, or CLSM).
5. Also report vertical bearing check (Table 1806.2 vertical pressure).
