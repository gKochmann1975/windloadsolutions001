How to Apply Zones
Zone 5 Any component that falls within the edge strip "a" distance from a building corner or edge. If any portion of the component is inside the edge strip area, the entire component is Zone 5.
Zone 4 A component that is fully interior — the entire opening must be more than "a" from all corners and edges. Measure from the building corner inward.
Example: With a = 4.0 ft, a window whose edge is 3 ft from the building corner is Zone 5 (within "a"). A window 6 ft from the corner is Zone 4 (fully beyond "a"). A window at 3.5 ft where part of it extends into the edge strip is still Zone 5. Tip: When in doubt, use Zone 5 — it gives the higher (more conservative) pressure, which is always safe.
