Pricing FAQ

Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?

No. Start your Building Intelligence Platform trial with just your email — no credit card. Add payment only when you're ready to subscribe. The free tier also gives you 3 searches per week with no signup commitment.

What happens after my free trial ends?

Your trial expires after 7 days. To keep using the platform, choose a paid plan — Starter, Pro, or Premium. Your trial data, saved searches, and settings are preserved when you upgrade.

What's the difference between Pro and Premium?

Pro includes 250 calculations per month with 5 concurrent users. Premium adds unlimited calculations, 10 concurrent users, built-in Hurricane Intelligence with natural-language questions, the hurricane path animator, priority support, and beta API access.

Can I change or cancel my plan later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime and the change takes effect immediately. Subscriptions are month-to-month or annual and can be cancelled anytime from your account.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) through our secure Stripe payment processor. Annual billing saves about 15% versus paying monthly.
