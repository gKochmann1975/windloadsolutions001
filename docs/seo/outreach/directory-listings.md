## Long description (~180 words — main "About" field)

> WindLoadCalc is browser-based wind load software for structural engineers,
> architects, and contractors. It automates ASCE 7-22 and ASCE 7-16 design wind
> pressures for Components & Cladding (windows, doors, shutters), the Main Wind
> Force Resisting System (MWFRS), and roof zones — and runs in any modern browser
> with nothing to install.
>
> Enter a project location and the software pulls the design wind speed
> automatically, including Florida High-Velocity Hurricane Zone and per-county
> values such as Miami-Dade and Collier. It supports Risk Categories I–IV and
> Exposure Categories B, C, and D, and exports submittal-ready reports as PDF,
> Excel (.xlsx), and CSV.
>
> Created in Naples, Florida in 2002 and online since 2006, WindLoadCalc was one
> of the first wind load tools an engineer could open in a browser — years ahead
> of newer cloud calculators. Beyond the software, an in-house Florida-licensed
> Professional Engineer offers a review-and-seal service for permit packages.
>
> Free trial available. Subscriptions from $14.99/month.
