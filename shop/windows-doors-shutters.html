One opening, three load checks

The mullion fails when you size for one unit but install two

A mulled assembly is not one calc. We check each tributary unit, the mullion carrying both tributary areas, and the wall opening itself.

  • Each tributary unit at its own effective wind area
  • The mullion — loaded by both units it joins
  • The wall opening the whole assembly sits in
  • Anchor demand sized for the real load path

Frequently asked questions

Which window, door, and shutter assemblies can I calculate?

Replacement and new-construction windows, sliding glass doors, French doors, entry and storm doors, plus accordion, roll-down, and panel hurricane shutters. Both impact-rated and non-impact products are supported as Components & Cladding.

How does it match design pressure to my product approval?

The calculator returns the required positive and negative C&C design pressures for each opening, then you compare them to the rated DP on the NOA or product approval. A clear pass or fail flags any opening whose approved DP is below the required pressure.

Does it handle mulled assemblies?

Yes. A mulled assembly is one opening with three load checks: each tributary unit, the mullion carrying both tributary areas, and the wall opening itself. Sizing anchors for a single unit when two are mulled is a common pullout-failure cause, so all three are checked.

Which ASCE 7 editions are supported?

ASCE 7-22, the current standard. Most jurisdictions are on 7-16 today, while 7-22 adoption is growing — confirm the adopted edition with your building department.

Does it support Florida HVHZ and Miami-Dade?

Yes. It applies Florida Building Code context including the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone for Miami-Dade and Broward, the wider wind-borne-debris region, and the 4-foot edge-strip minimum required in Florida. Miami-Dade NOA matching is built in.

What do I get in the Engineering Report?

A permit-ready Engineering Report with design wind speed, exposure category, topographic and elevation factors, zone diagrams, per-opening required pressures, and DP-to-approval comparisons. Export to PDF, Excel, CSV, or print — with optional company branding.

Can I get the report signed and sealed by a P.E.?

Yes. PE sign-and-seal is available in all 50 states through our PE network. Florida work up to three stories is handled by our in-house Florida P.E.; projects elsewhere are routed to a licensed engineer in that state. Sealing is a separate professional service from the software.

Are these reports accepted by building departments?

Our reports carry a 100% permit-approval record across 24 years. Building officials recognize the professional format and complete ASCE 7 documentation, and PE sign-and-seal is available on request.

Do I need an engineering degree to use it?

No. It is built for engineers, contractors, installers, and product specialists. The step-by-step interface guides each input with tooltips and built-in ASCE 7 references. Understanding basic C&C concepts helps when interpreting results.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from your account dashboard. Access continues through the end of your paid billing period, with no cancellation fees and a 7-day money-back guarantee on new subscriptions.
