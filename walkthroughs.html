Walkthrough questions

Is a walkthrough a video or the real software?

It is the real software. A walkthrough opens the actual ASCE 7-22 calculator in your browser, pre-loaded with a verified sample building, and puts a moving spotlight on each field as you go. You see genuine pressures and a real report — nothing is a screen recording.

Do I need an account to launch a walkthrough?

No. Every walkthrough opens without a login, a card, or a meter. It runs a read-only guided example so you can judge the workflow before you ever start a trial.

Which calculators have a walkthrough today?

Windows, Doors & Shutters is the most complete tour. Guided walkthroughs are also live for MWFRS directional and envelope buildings, C&C roofs, signs and billboards, rooftop equipment, chimneys and tanks, trussed towers, and freestanding walls — all built on ASCE 7-22.
