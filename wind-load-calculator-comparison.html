Comparison FAQ

Is this wind load calculator ASCE 7-22 compliant?

Yes. It uses official ASCE 7-22 wind speed maps, Florida Building Code 2023, and verified local amendments — every calculation is permit-ready, with each coefficient traceable to its ASCE 7-22 section.

How does it compare to manual calculation?

A typical project takes 2–15 minutes versus 4–8 hours by hand. It auto-looks-up wind speed, performs the logarithmic GCp interpolation, and eliminates the manual interpolation errors that get permits rejected.

Is there a free version?

Yes — the free wind load calculator returns the ASCE 7-22 design wind speed for any U.S. ZIP, with Florida Building Code overrides, no signup. For multi-component schedules and permit-ready reports you need a paid subscription.

Do the reports come PE-stamped?

The software produces permit-ready Engineering Reports with full coefficient citations. PE sign-and-seal is a separate service, available for Florida projects (single-family residential, up to 3 stories) on request.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $35/month, with annual billing saving about 20%. Every plan includes a 7-day free trial with no credit card required.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are month-to-month or annual and can be cancelled anytime, with a 7-day money-back guarantee.
