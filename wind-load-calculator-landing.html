Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be an engineer to use this?

No. The calculator is designed for engineers, architects, designers, and contractors. The interface guides you through each step with clear explanations and built-in ASCE 7-22 code references.

Will this work in my state?

Yes — it works for all locations in the USA and its territories. We have special certification modules for Florida (FL# Product Approval, Miami-Dade NOA), Louisiana, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Hawaii. All other states use standard ASCE 7-22 provisions.

Are these reports accepted by building departments?

Our reports have a 100% permit approval rate across 24 years of operation. Building officials recognize and trust our professional format and complete documentation, and PE sign-and-seal is available for Florida projects on request.

How long does it take to learn?

Most users complete their first project within an hour. The intuitive step-by-step interface guides you through each calculation. Video tutorials are available if needed, but most find them unnecessary.

What happens when building codes change?

Automatic updates are included with your subscription. When new code provisions are released, we update the calculator. You always have access to the latest ASCE standards at no additional cost.

Can I try it before subscribing?

Yes — a 7-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required. Complete real projects and export reports during the trial to verify it meets your needs.

Do you provide support?

Yes — all plans include email support from licensed structural engineers with a guaranteed 24-hour response time. We provide code interpretation guidance and help with complex projects. Phone support is available with premium plans.

Can I export my calculations?

Yes — export to PDF, Excel, CSV, or print from any local printer. Unlimited exports are included — no per-report fees. Add your company logo for professional branding. Reports include an executive summary and detailed calculations.

Is my data secure?

Your projects are stored securely in the cloud with strong encryption. Multi-device access means you can work from desktop, tablet, or phone. We never share your data with third parties.

What's your refund policy?

A 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied for any reason, we'll refund your subscription — no questions asked. You can also cancel anytime with no penalties or long-term commitments.

Can I subscribe to multiple categories?

Yes — each category is a separate subscription. Subscribe to Windows + Roofs + Solar, or just the categories you need. Mix and match based on your project types. Bundle pricing will be available soon.

What makes you different from competitors?

Three key differentiators: (1) automatic wind speed lookup from the project location saves hours, (2) state-specific certification modules (FL#, NOA, etc.) that no competitor offers, and (3) product certification management with automatic compliance verification — plus 24 years of proven results.
